Transfer bookmarks

  1. Select bookmarks or folders in Export. Arrange them with the arrows and save your file as HTML or JSON.
  2. Copy the file to the other computer and open it in Import. Choose the destinations and review matching URLs.
  3. Apply the import. History lets you undo additions and updates in this browser profile.

Bookmark Bridge processes files on your device. Exported files contain bookmark titles and URLs.

Undo this import?

Undo restores the previous details of updated bookmarks and removes items added by the import. Bookmarks you edited or moved afterward and folders with additional contents are preserved.
